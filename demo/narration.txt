Spike Bench is a neurophysiology workbench in the browser: Hodgkin Huxley current and voltage clamp, an S T D P microcircuit, f I sweeps, phase portraits and S H A 256 verified exports.
Everything integrates locally in double precision, with deterministic seeds and zero dependencies.

The clamp tab drives the squid axon model with fourth order Runge Kutta integration.
Six presets cover resting, single spike, train, T T X and T E A channel blocks and voltage clamp.
The membrane trace draws with its stimulus underneath while the stat rail reports spike count, rate, peak, half width and afterhyperpolarization.

Blocking sodium channels with T T X abolishes the spikes while leak and capacitance remain.
That is the classic pharmacology result, computed live in the page.

The S T D P sandbox wires up to sixty presynaptic inputs into one leaky integrate and fire neuron.
Every pre post pairing updates its synapse inside a causal window: early pre spikes potentiate, late ones depress.
Weight trajectories, the delta t correlation and the raster plot make the learning visible, and a fixed seed reproduces the run bit for bit.

The f I sweep bisects the rheobase and charts firing rate against injected current, with a monotonicity check.
The phase portrait plots the trajectory against exact nullclines and equilibria for the Izhikevich phenotypes and a reduced Hodgkin Huxley model.

The lab tab exports the session as a canonical J S O N document with a pure JavaScript S H A 256 checksum.
A bookmark hash restores the whole session, and a seventeen check battery verifies R K 4 convergence, S T D P sign symmetry, rheobase monotonicity and the codecs.
